Nihongo Michi 日本語の道

A free app that teaches real-world Japanese from zero to JLPT N1, on Windows, iPhone and Android.

  • 123 lessons, 420 grammar points and 75 role-play conversations
  • Hiragana, katakana and all 2,230 JLPT kanji with stroke order
  • Spaced-repetition reviews, listening and speaking practice
  • Optional sync of your progress through your own Google Drive
